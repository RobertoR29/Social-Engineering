# Social Engineering: Scattered Spider & the MGM Resorts Breach

## Overview

This project is a case study analysis of the 2023 cyberattack on MGM Resorts International,
carried out by the threat actor group Scattered Spider. The attack began with a 10-minute
vishing call to MGM's IT help desk using employee information sourced from LinkedIn, resulting
in roughly $100 million in damages, a 10-day operational outage, and the compromise of
millions of customer records. The study profiles Scattered Spider, documents their full attack
lifecycle from OSINT reconnaissance through ALPHV/BlackCat ransomware deployment, evaluates
MGM's defensive failures and incident response, and concludes with actionable recommendations
covering phishing-resistant MFA, Zero Trust architecture, hardened cloud identity controls,
and social engineering simulation programs.
